— 03 / The Ritual

Three slow movements each morning.

We believe the most nourishing rituals are the simplest. Tear a sachet. Pour warm water. Stir with intention. JAGAVE fits inside the eight minutes between waking and the day.

— Step 01

Tear

Open one 10g sachet along the perforation.

— Step 02

Pour

Add 150ml of warm water or steamed milk.

— Step 03

Sip

Stir for eight seconds. Enjoy the morning glow.

— 06 / Story

A small studio in Kyoto & Bengaluru.

JAGAVE began as a quiet conversation between two friends — a perfumer in Kyoto and a barista in Bengaluru — about the eight minutes between waking and the day. We thought a slow, intentional morning could be the most nourishing ritual of all.

Every sachet is composed in small batches, packaged by hand, and shipped within twenty-four hours. We work directly with single-origin farms in Coorg and bio-fermenters in Japan — because the most nourishing rituals begin with the most thoughtful sources.
